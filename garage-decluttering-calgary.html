What Moves the Price of a Declutter

We quote each garage instead of publishing a rate card, because two double garages with the same amount of stuff can be very different jobs. These are the things we look at in your photos.

How much leaves, not how much is there

A packed garage where you keep 70 percent is mostly sorting time. A half-full garage that's going 90 percent to the landfill is mostly truck space and tipping fees. City of Calgary landfills charge by weight, with a flat fee for small loads, so heavy stuff like old tile, concrete pavers and broken treadmills costs more to dispose of than its volume suggests.

The hazardous shelf

Every garage has one: half cans of paint from three renovations ago, a jerry can of stale gas, deck stain, pool chemicals, the propane tank from a barbecue that's long gone. The City's household hazardous waste drop-offs take these free, but they limit how much one vehicle can bring per visit, so a big shelf can mean more than one run.

Access

A front-drive double in Evanston or Mahogany has a driveway we can sort onto and back a truck up to. A detached garage off a back lane in Mount Royal, Altadore or Bridgeland usually doesn't. The lane is shared, so we sort inside and load straight to the truck, which is slower. Snow and an unploughed lane in January slow it further.

How fast you decide

This is the biggest variable and the one you control. Someone who walks in with a never-touch list and a rule for the kids' old gear finishes in half the time of someone deciding each hockey stick on its merits. The prep list further down gets you most of the way.

Whether you want shelving after

Decluttering ends with an empty floor and your keep pile grouped by category. Shelving, wall systems and cabinets for what stays are a separate job with its own quote through our garage organization team. Plenty of people stop at the declutter, and that's fine.

Keep-or-Go Rules for the Things Calgary Garages Collect

Judging items one at a time is what makes decluttering take forever. Decide a rule per category before the day and most of the pile sorts itself. These are the categories that show up in nearly every garage we see here, with the rule and where the "go" pile actually goes.

Illustration, not a client's garage.Count the categories here: paint cans, bikes, a mower, lumber offcuts, bags of clothes, camping gear. Each one gets a rule before declutter day.

Category rules and where each "go" pile ends up
Category A rule that works Where the "go" pile goes
Winter tires Keep one set per vehicle you still own. Check the four-digit date code on the sidewall (week and year); many tire makers recommend replacing tires somewhere around the 6 to 10 year mark regardless of tread. Tire recycling, or sold if they fit a common rim and have tread left
Snowblower Keep it if it started last winter. A second one, or one that hasn't run in two seasons, goes. Sold in fall, or scrap metal once the fuel is drained
Kids' bikes, skates, hockey gear Keep what fits a child in the house now. Everything else leaves before it's two sizes behind. Donation if usable; broken bikes to scrap metal
Car seats and drop-side cribs Car seats have an expiry date on the shell; past it, they go. Drop-side cribs have been banned from sale in Canada since 2016, so they can't be sold or given away. Recycling or landfill, never donation
Leftover paint Keep one labelled can per room you'd touch up. Dried-out, unlabelled and ex-house colours go. City of Calgary household hazardous waste drop-off (free)
Propane, fuel, chemicals Keep only what goes with equipment you still own and use. City hazardous waste drop-off; small propane cylinders accepted
Renovation leftovers Keep spare flooring or tile only if it matches something still in the house. Habitat for Humanity ReStore if new and unopened; otherwise landfill by weight
Old fridge or chest freezer If it isn't plugged in and running, it goes. Garage freezers that sit through a Calgary winter often aren't worth saving. Landfill, which charges a refrigerant removal fee per appliance
Duplicate tools Keep the best one of each. Inherited sets you'll never use can be sold or offered to family first. Sell pile, donation, or family
Cardboard boxes on the slab Open every one. Anything worth keeping moves into a bin off the floor; the cardboard goes. Blue cart or recycling depot

That last row is a Calgary problem in particular. Every chinook melts the snow packed into your wheel wells and it runs across the slab. Boxes sitting on the concrete soak it up from the bottom, then freeze when the cold comes back. By spring the bottom box in the stack has collapsed and whatever was in it is damp.

If You Want to Sell It, Time It to the Season

A sell pile left in the corner until spring still takes up the parking spot. Calgary buyers on Facebook Marketplace and Kijiji shop for seasonal gear right before they need it, so the month you declutter decides what's worth listing.

October and November

Snowblowers, winter tires on rims, skis, skates and kids' hockey gear sell fastest now, before the first big snow. Declutter in late October and list these the same week.

March to May

Bikes, lawn mowers, patio furniture, camping gear and pressure washers. Spring also fills the garage with grit and gravel off the roads, which is why a lot of people pair this with a spring garage cleanout.

Off-season

Snowblowers in July and bikes in December sit unsold. If the season is wrong, donating now usually beats storing it for six months in the space you just cleared.

On declutter day we set your sell pile aside in one spot and tell you what's in it. You list it. If you'd rather not deal with selling at all, say so on the day and it joins the donation run.

Twenty Minutes of Prep That Halves Your Part

If you want your garage decluttered quickly, most of the time saved comes from these five things. None of them involve lifting anything.

1

Write the never-touch list

The things nobody in the house will agree to part with. Write it down so it's settled before we arrive and nobody re-argues it on the day.

2

Pick your category rules

Go through the table above and decide your version of each rule with whoever shares the garage. Ten minutes of agreement saves an hour of debate over the camping stove.

3

Move the car out and clear the driveway

On a front-drive house the driveway is our sorting floor. In winter, shovel it, or the lane behind a detached garage.

4

Tell us about the hazardous shelf and the heavy stuff

Roughly how much paint and how many propane tanks, plus anything over 100 pounds like a freezer, a safe or a pile of patio pavers. It changes the truck we send and the number of drop-off runs.

5

Name one decider

If two people share the garage, agree who has the final say on the maybe pile. Couples deciding together is fine; couples deciding separately and then reversing each other is where hours go.

Garage Decluttering in Calgary: Questions Before Booking

How much does it cost to declutter and organize a garage in Calgary?

Decluttering and organizing are priced separately. A declutter is quoted on how much leaves the garage, how many hazardous items need City drop-offs, access to the garage, and whether you want a sell pile handled. Shelving or wall systems for what stays are a separate organization quote. Send three photos and we quote the declutter before anyone books a truck.

How can I declutter my garage quickly?

Make the decisions in one sitting instead of over many weekends. Pull the car out, decide your never-touch list in advance, and have someone else do the carrying and the drop-off runs. With a crew sorting in front of you, most of your part takes a few hours.

How do I decide what to keep in my garage?

Use a rule per category rather than judging each item. In Calgary that means keeping one set of winter tires with a recent date code, one of each tool, and the sports gear someone used in the last full season. Expired car seats and drop-side cribs can't be passed on, so they go to recycling. Anything you can't decide on goes in one labelled maybe box with a date on it.

Do I need to be home while you declutter my garage?

For the deciding, yes. Nobody else can tell which box is your late father's tools and which is old wire. Once the keep, sell, donate and drop-off piles are set, you can leave. The carrying, loading, drop-off runs and sweep-out don't need you.

Where can seniors in Calgary get free hoarding help?

Sage Seniors Association runs This Full House for adults 55 and over who struggle with hoarding, and Elements Calgary Mental Health Centre runs a decluttering program for adults. The City of Calgary's Home Services for Seniors program ended on September 30, 2026; Alberta Supports can tell you whether the province's Special Needs Assistance for Seniors program applies. GarageScape is a paid service; if the problem is hoarding rather than a busy household, start with those programs and call us for the physical clear-out when the person is ready.

How long does a Calgary garage declutter take?

Most garages take 4 to 8 hours on site, depending on how much is in there and how quickly decisions get made. A double garage where the owner has already pulled out the obvious junk is near the short end. A garage that has absorbed a downsized parent's household is near the long end.

What is the difference between decluttering and junk removal?

A junk hauler takes what you point at. Decluttering covers the pointing: we sort every item with you into keep, sell, donate, recycle and drop-off piles, then deliver each pile where it belongs, including the paint, propane and tires most haulers won't put on the truck.

What happens to old paint, propane tanks and tires from my garage?

They never go in your black cart. Paint, solvents, small propane cylinders and other household hazardous waste go to a City of Calgary household hazardous waste drop-off, which is free. Tires and scrap metal go to recycling. Old fridges and freezers go to the landfill, which charges a refrigerant removal fee.
