Our Harmony & Elbow Valley Garage Transformation Process

1

Measure & Design

We measure the garage precisely — every wall, the tall estate ceilings, the door swings, windows, the man-door, and the mechanical room that eats into a corner. In a triple or quad garage that means mapping all the bays so storage lands where it works and every vehicle still fits, all before a cabinet is built.

2

Declutter & Sort

Before installation we clear the backlog — the outgrown lake toys, the boxes that never made it into the house, the gear from three seasons ago. Keep, donate, sell, dispose, handled with calm, judgment-free guidance, with usable items routed to donation rather than the landfill.

3

Floor First

If your project includes a floor coating, it goes in while the garage is empty. On a Springbank estate slab that means a proper grind, crack repair where needed, then a polyaspartic or epoxy system chosen to shrug off road salt, gravel dust, hot tires, dripping lake gear, and chinook freeze-thaw.

4

Install Cabinets, Gear Walls & Overhead

Full-height cabinet towers for closed storage, slatwall and dedicated gear walls for the lake, golf, and mountain kit, and overhead racks above the doors for seasonal bins — all lifted off the slab and planned so every vehicle still parks and every door still opens.

5

Walkthrough & Handover

We label, walk you through the layout, and leave a garage that stays organized because everything has an obvious place — including a simple plan for swapping the summer lake gear and the winter kit as the seasons turn out on the ridge.

The Harmony & Elbow Valley Garage Challenge — and How We Solve It

Two Lake Communities, One Storage Problem

Harmony and Elbow Valley are different in age and layout — Harmony is a new, resort-style master plan around a lake, a beach club, and Mickelson National, while Elbow Valley is an established estate community wrapped around Elbow Valley, Clearwater, and the trout-stocked Fisherman's Lake — but they share the same garage story. Both are lake-lifestyle communities where the home is large, the garage is a triple or quad, and the household's toys multiply to match the water at the end of the street. A big garage feels like it can absorb anything, so it does, until the vehicles are the thing living outside. What is missing is never space; it is a plan, and that is exactly the gap we close.

The Big-Garage Paradox

It is the same paradox we see across Springbank and Rocky View County: the bigger the garage, the more likely the cars are parked outside. A triple or quad estate garage starts with every intention of housing three or four vehicles, but with no built-in storage the space fills organically over the years — lake gear along one wall, golf bags in a corner, a deep-freeze, seasonal decor, a workbench that grew, and the overflow that has no home inside the house. We reverse it by building up the walls and overhead so the floor is freed: the toys get a real place, the workshop gets defined, and the bays go back to being bays for vehicles, sheltered from the Springbank winter.

Life on the Water — Storing the Lake Fleet

This is the angle that sets these communities apart from anywhere else we work. When your neighbourhood is built around a lake and a beach club, the garage becomes the boathouse: paddleboards, kayaks, canoes, wakeboards, and the occasional e-foil, plus life jackets, wetsuits, towels, beach chairs, and a fishing kit for Fisherman's Lake. Left loose, all of it leans against a wall, drips on the floor, and buries whatever is behind it. We plan a proper lake wall — vertical racks and ceiling hoists for the big boards and boats, wall hooks for jackets and paddles, a drip-and-dry zone so wet gear is not soaking your cabinets, and labelled bins for the small stuff — so the whole family can grab what they need and be at the water in minutes, then put it all back in seconds.

A Golf Community's Garage

Harmony is anchored by Mickelson National Golf Club and the Launch Pad, and Elbow Valley sits minutes from Elbow Springs and the Glencoe courses — so a good share of local garages are really half golf lockers. Bags lean where they land, shoes and range gear scatter, and a set of clubs takes a surprising amount of floor once you add a pull cart and a second family bag. We build dedicated bag stands, cabinet space for shoes, balls, gloves, and accessories, and a clean, protected staging spot right by the door, so the clubs load into the cart or vehicle without knocking around and store safely up off the slab when the season closes.

Ridge Weather, Gravel Dust, and Long Driveways

Out on the Springbank ridge, west of the city's shelter, the garages take the chinooks head-on — that dramatic winter swing from deep cold to above freezing and back that is so hard on concrete — along with gravel dust off county roads and long driveways, and a steady load of road salt and grit tracked in from Highway 8 and the Trans-Canada. Add dripping lake gear in summer and a bare slab pits, dusts, and stains fast. A ground-and-coated polyaspartic or epoxy floor seals the concrete against all of it, brightens the space, and turns the annual salt-and-dust cleanup into a quick squeegee.

Acreage, Detached Shops, and County Living

Not every Springbank property is a lakefront lot. The areas around Elbow Valley and through Springbank include acreage homes with detached shops, RV and trailer parking, and the equipment that comes with maintaining land — mowers, snow-clearing gear, and seasonal tools. Where a home has both an attached estate garage and a separate shop, we plan them together: the attached garage for vehicles, everyday gear, and finish-level cabinetry; the shop zoned for the messy, oversized, and seasonal work. Each building ends up with a clear job instead of both slowly becoming the same overflow pile.

Resale in a Premium West-of-City Market

Harmony and Elbow Valley are among the most sought-after addresses west of Calgary, and buyers at this level expect every space to be finished and considered — the garage included. A blank builder garage stacked with totes, or an older estate garage with tired shelving, reads as unfinished against the rest of the home. A clean coated floor and built-in cabinetry photograph as a genuine feature and signal that the whole property has been well cared for. On a premium Springbank listing, a garage that shows like the rest of the estate is a quiet but real edge.

Frequently Asked Questions About Garage Organization in Harmony & Elbow Valley

Do you organize the large estate garages in Harmony and Elbow Valley?

That is most of what we do out here. Harmony and Elbow Valley estate homes come with triple and even quad garages, and while the square footage is generous, that is exactly why they fill up — a big empty garage invites a decade of lake toys, golf bags, seasonal decor, and project overflow with no plan behind it. We map the whole footprint at once: full-height cabinet towers along the back and side walls, slatwall and dedicated gear walls for the equipment you touch every week, overhead racks above the doors for off-season bins, and a defined workshop or drop zone — all sized so every vehicle still parks inside through a Springbank winter.

We have a lot of lake gear — how do you store paddleboards, kayaks and beach kit?

Lake gear is the signature storage challenge of these communities, and it is our favourite part of a Harmony or Elbow Valley project. With Harmony's lake and beach club and Elbow Valley's Elbow Valley, Clearwater and Fisherman's lakes right in the neighbourhood, families accumulate paddleboards, kayaks, canoes, wakeboards, life jackets, wetsuits, beach chairs, and fishing kit. We build vertical racks and ceiling hoists for the big boards and boats, a drip-and-dry zone for wet gear so it is not dripping on your cabinets, and labelled bins for the small stuff, so the whole family can load up for a lake afternoon in minutes and put it all back without a pile in the corner.

Can you build golf storage for Mickelson National and the local clubs?

Yes. Harmony is built around Mickelson National Golf Club and the Launch Pad, and Elbow Valley sits minutes from Elbow Springs and the Glencoe golf courses, so a lot of local garages are really half golf lockers. We add dedicated bag stands, cabinet space for shoes, balls, and accessories, and a clean, protected spot near the door so clubs are grabbed and loaded without knocking around loose gear — and stored safely off the slab in the off-season.

What does garage organization cost in Harmony or Elbow Valley?

Every estate garage is quoted individually. A decluttering and organization project starts around $199, while a full transformation with custom cabinetry, slatwall, dedicated gear walls, overhead racks, and an epoxy or polyaspartic floor is a larger investment scoped to your garage's size, condition, and finish level. Larger triple and quad garages naturally involve more material and labour, but they also reward a complete system the most. We always start with a free, no-obligation consultation and a written quote.

Is an epoxy or polyaspartic floor worth it on a Springbank estate garage?

For a lake-estate garage west of the city, absolutely. Out here on the exposed Springbank ridge the garages take the full chinook freeze-thaw swing, plus gravel dust, and a steady load of road salt and grit tracked in from Highway 8, the Trans-Canada, and long driveways. Do you serve acreage lots and homes with detached shops in Springbank?

We do. Alongside the lake communities, Springbank and the areas around Elbow Valley include acreage lots and estate homes with detached shops, RV parking, and longer driveways.
