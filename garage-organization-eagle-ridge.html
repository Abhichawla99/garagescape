The Landlocked Lot Problem

98 Lots, Fixed Since 1960

Eagle Ridge Drive and its handful of side streets were platted out once, in 1960, on a strip of land the reservoir surrounds on three sides. No new lots have been added since, and none can be, because the boundary is the water on one side and 14 Street W on the other. That means every garage in the community sits on a footprint that was decided over sixty years ago.

A Value Mismatch That Shows Up in the Garage

Original 1960s homes here have been renovated or rebuilt at a much higher standard than when the community was built, but the garage that came with the lot often didn't grow to match. A double bay sized for two mid-century sedans now has to hold two modern SUVs, reservoir gear, and golf equipment, with zero room to add a third bay because there's nowhere left on the lot to put one.

Vertical and Full-Height Systems Do the Work

Since the floor plate can't expand, we build up instead: full-height cabinetry to the ceiling, slatwall that uses wall space a shelf never touches, and overhead platforms for the gear that only comes out twice a year. It's the same logic we bring to compact garages in Mount Royal, adapted for a reservoir-side lot instead of a rear lane.

Reservoir Pathway Gear & Golf Storage

The Reservoir Is Right There

Eagle Ridge backs directly onto the Glenmore Reservoir, with pathway access into North Glenmore Park. That puts bikes, paddleboards, and kayaks in a lot of Eagle Ridge garages, usually leaned against a wall or blocking half the parking bay because nobody's built them a proper spot.

Wall and Overhead Storage for Pathway Gear

Bikes go on vertical wall hooks at grabbing height, paddleboards and kayaks on wall cradles or an overhead sling through the off-season, and helmets and life jackets in a bin by the door. The goal is gear that's ready for a pathway ride in under a minute, not a dig through the garage first.

Earl Grey Golf Club Storage

Earl Grey Golf Club sits just north of the community, and golf bags are a fixture in a lot of Eagle Ridge garages. A wall-mounted vertical rack near the interior door keeps clubs off the floor and out of the way of the car, without eating into any real storage capacity.

Rebuilding in Eagle Ridge? Get the Garage Right Before Drywall

Teardowns Are Common Here

With only 98 properties and land this scarce against the reservoir, a full teardown-rebuild is one of the more common ways an Eagle Ridge home changes hands and changes shape. The garage usually gets rebuilt too, and that's the single best moment to plan it properly.

What We Confirm Before the Walls Close

We meet with you and your builder while the garage is still framed, not finished. That's when we place blocking in the studs for cabinetry and slatwall, confirm outlet and conduit locations for EV charging, and plan the epoxy floor prep sequence, so the finished garage doesn't need a single wall opened up again to fix something we could have planned for.

Frequently Asked Questions About Garage Organization in Eagle Ridge

How much does garage organization cost in Eagle Ridge?

Garage organization in Eagle Ridge starts at $199 for a standard garage, with full estate systems and custom cabinetry running higher depending on garage size, finish level, and whether the project is a retrofit or part of a rebuild. We provide a free, no-obligation quote before any work begins.

Do you work on original 1960s Eagle Ridge homes, or only the new builds?

Both, regularly. Eagle Ridge is a mix of original mid-century homes from the 1960s and newer multi-million-dollar rebuilds, and the garages need different approaches. Original garages usually need smarter vertical storage to work with a smaller footprint. New builds get a full system designed and installed from a blank slab.

Can you plan the garage before drywall goes up on a rebuild?

Yes, and it's the best time to call us. We work with your builder before drywall closes the walls to place blocking for cabinetry, confirm EV charging conduit and outlet locations, and plan the epoxy floor prep, so nothing has to be retrofitted or drilled into finished walls later.

Can you build storage for bikes and paddleboards for the reservoir pathway?

Yes. Eagle Ridge backs onto the Glenmore Reservoir pathway system, and we build wall-mounted bike racks and overhead or wall cradles for paddleboards and kayaks, positioned so gear comes down for pathway season and stores clean the rest of the year.

Can you fit golf storage for Earl Grey Golf Club members?

Yes. A wall-mounted golf bag rack near the door to the house is one of the most requested additions for Eagle Ridge clients who golf at Earl Grey, and it takes almost no floor space to install.

My Eagle Ridge garage is smaller than the rest of the house. Can you still make it work?

This is one of the most common calls we get in Eagle Ridge. Because the community was built out on a fixed lot pattern in 1960, many original garages are modest double bays attached to homes that have since been renovated or rebuilt at a much higher finish level. We use full-height cabinetry, slatwall, and overhead platforms to get estate-level storage capacity out of a footprint that can't physically grow.

How long does an Eagle Ridge garage organization project take?

Most single-visit organization and shelving projects take 4 to 8 hours. Full custom cabinetry builds, especially ones coordinated with a rebuild timeline, usually run as a separate install once the epoxy floor and finishes are ready. We confirm the schedule in your free consultation.

Do you install custom cabinets and epoxy flooring too?

Yes. Beyond decluttering and shelving, we install custom cabinetry and coordinate epoxy flooring, matching the finish level of an Eagle Ridge estate home rather than a standard garage build. See our storage solutions and estate and vacation-home pages for what a full outfit looks like.
