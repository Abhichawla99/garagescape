November 1: The Trailer Comes Off the Driveway

The City of Calgary regulates RV parking under its Community Standards and Traffic Bylaws, and its definition of an RV covers motor homes, trailers, campers and boats. The rules change on November 1. Fines run from $400 to $10,000.

Will It Fit? Measure Before We Empty Anything

Clearing a bay and then finding the boat is 30 centimetres too tall for the door is an expensive afternoon. Before we book a cleanout that's meant to make room for a trailer, we ask for these. Measure with the garage door open.

  1. Door opening height and width, measured at the lowest point: the bottom of the open door panel or the opener rail, not the header trim.
  2. The trailer's overall length with the tongue and hitch, the boat's outboard motor or swim platform, and any bike rack or spare tire mounted on the back. Compare it with the bay depth from the inside of the door to the back wall, minus any step up into the house.
  3. Height on the trailer: windshield, bimini frame, tower, roof vents or an air conditioner on a camper. This is the measurement that most often rules a garage out.
  4. What hangs from the ceiling. Overhead racks, a storage lift, the opener motor and its rail, and lights all take height out of the middle of the bay. A rack full of bins can be the difference.
  5. Posts and steps. A support post, a furnace or water heater closet, or a step down from the mudroom door narrows the bay at one end.
  6. How you'll back it in. A driveway that slopes up or down to the garage, a curve, or a neighbour's parked car changes the turn. If the turn is tight, the bay needs to be empty wall to wall, not just down the middle.

If the numbers say it won't fit, we'll say so before we start, and the cleanout can still be worth doing for the cars. Our crew doesn't tow, back up trailers or drive your vehicles, so we leave the bay empty and swept for you or the storage company to bring the trailer in.

Everything Leaves by the Front Driveway

In an inner-city house with a lane garage, the truck backs up to the garage door out of sight. Here the truck parks on your driveway, in full view of the crescent, and every load crosses the front of the house. That changes how we run the day.

  1. The truck goes on your driveway, not the street. The driveway is private property, so there's no City street use permit involved, and the street stays clear for neighbours. If your driveway is short or steep, we'll ask for a photo and may park at the curb for loading only.
  2. No pile on the driveway overnight. Everything we bring out goes on the truck the same day. Donations and keep items are staged inside the garage, not on the lawn.
  3. Cart day. City rules say carts go out no earlier than 7 p.m. the night before, must be out by 7 a.m., and come back in by 7 p.m. If your street's collection day falls on the job, we work around the carts and wheel yours back in when we leave. Green cart collection goes to every other week from the week of November 2.
  4. School traffic. West Springs Elementary, West Ridge School and Ernest Manning High School are all in these communities. If you're on a drop-off route, we time the truck's arrival for after the morning rush.
  5. Ice on a sloped driveway. Some houses here sit on hillside lots with driveways that slope to the garage. From November on we bring ice melt and lay mats at the door. We'd rather book a warmer day than carry a freezer down a glazed drive.
  6. Through the house only if you ask. Some things live in the basement or the mudroom closet and need to come out through the house. We cover floors and door frames before anything moves.

The nearest City landfills are on the northwest and southeast sides of the city, so from the west end every load is a long drive. We sort as we go, so one trip covers the landfill, the hazardous waste drop-off and the donation stop, and you're not paying for repeat runs.

Five People, One Garage: How the Sorting Works

In a big household, nobody owns the whole garage. The hockey bag belongs to a teenager who isn't home, the road bike belongs to a parent who still means to ride it, and the car seats belong to whichever grandparent asked to keep them. A cleanout stalls when we hold up every item for the one person who can decide. So we sort in two passes.

  1. First pass, with whoever is home: everything that's clearly garbage, recycling, hazardous waste or a shared household item goes straight to its pile. This is usually most of the garage.
  2. Second pass, by person: anything that belongs to someone specific goes into a named pile or bin on one side of the garage. We photograph each pile and send the photos to you to share, so the people who aren't home can decide by message.
  3. One deadline: what nobody claims by the end of the day goes to donation. Sports gear, bikes and kids' equipment in good shape almost never have to go to landfill.

Car seats are the exception. Transport Canada says not to use a car seat past its expiry date, and the Government of Canada's second-hand advice says not to use one whose history is unknown, so we don't donate them; they go to recycling or landfill. Tires from seasonal swaps can't go in a black cart: the City doesn't collect automobile waste, including tires and batteries. Many tire shops take old tires back when you book your next swap, or we can take them.

Questions From Aspen Woods, Springbank Hill and West Springs

Can I park my RV or trailer on my driveway in winter in Calgary?

No. Under the City of Calgary's Community Standards and Traffic Bylaws, RVs (motor homes, trailers, campers and boats) can't be parked on a front driveway from November 1 to March 31. From April 1 to October 31 you can park one on a front driveway for three consecutive days, then it has to be gone for at least 48 hours. Fines range from $400 to $10,000.

Where can I keep a boat or trailer in Calgary over the winter?

Inside your garage, in a side or back yard, or off-site in storage. The City's Land Use Bylaw allows an RV in a side yard setback or back yard with no time limit, as long as it doesn't block intersection sight lines. A motorized RV can sit on the street in front of your house for up to three days, then must be off-site for 48 hours. Detached trailers and campers can't be parked on City streets or alleys at all.

How much does it cost to clean out a garage in Calgary?

We quote from photos or a visit, because volume isn't the only thing that moves the price. In a front-drive house the carry is short, so the bigger factors are how much goes to landfill by weight, items with their own fees, how much sorting needs a family member present, and whether the job has to be done by a fixed date. City of Calgary landfill rates in 2026 are $113 a tonne for garbage, with a $25 flat fee for loads under 250 kg, and paint and chemicals go free to household hazardous waste drop-offs, up to 20 litres per visit.

How much does it cost to use the dump in Calgary?

City of Calgary landfills charge $113 a tonne for basic garbage in 2026, with a $25 flat fee for loads under 250 kg. Separated drywall and clean untreated wood are $90 a tonne, scrap metal is $113 a tonne, and a fridge or freezer carries a $17 refrigerant charge. Household hazardous waste such as paint is free at City drop-offs, up to 20 litres per visit. The City's landfills are in the northwest and southeast; none are in the west end.

How do I get rid of garage clutter?

Decide what the garage has to hold first, such as a trailer by November 1 or two cars, and measure for it. Then sort by destination rather than by shelf: keep, the house's builder leftovers, donate, City hazardous waste, recycling and landfill. Give each person in the house their own pile to decide on, and haul everything the same day so nothing drifts back onto the shelves. A black cart takes about four bags a week, so a whole garage needs a truck or a landfill trip.

How often should you clean the garage?

In Calgary, twice a year works for most houses. A fall cleanout before November makes room for the cars and anything that comes off the driveway for winter. A spring clean after the last road salt and gravel washes off what the tires tracked in. A full cleanout like the one on this page is usually needed every few years, or before a move.

Do I need to be home for the garage cleanout?

Only for the walkthrough, which can be in person or on a video call. On the day we work with the overhead door code you give us, leave anything that looks personal or valuable in a set-aside pile for you instead of deciding, and send photos when we finish.

Will you move the boat or trailer into the garage when you're done?

No. Our crew doesn't tow, back up trailers or drive clients' vehicles. We clear and sweep the bay, check the measurements you gave us against the empty space, and leave it ready for you or your storage company to back the trailer in.
