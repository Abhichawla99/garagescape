Where Does the Bin Go?

A bin for a garage cleanout usually lands in front of the garage you're trying to empty. On a front driveway, that means the cars can't get in or out until the bin is collected, and every load gets carried around the side of it. Behind the house, on a lane, it means the City is involved.

The City of Calgary requires a street use permit to use any City road right-of-way, and it spells out what that covers: a street, a sidewalk, an alley, a walkway, a back lane or a boulevard. Placing a garbage bin is on its list of reasons for needing one. The permit is $31.50 a day plus GST, you apply online through myID, and the City asks for at least five business days for a lower-impact closure. Its page also says a separate hoarding permit can be needed if the closure lasts more than 24 hours or runs into weekday rush hours. Permits are issued at the Traffic Engineer's discretion under the Streets Bylaw.

That matters most in the older communities where the garage faces the lane, like Mount Royal and Elbow Park, Lakeview and Altadore and Marda Loop. It also matters in November, because the City doesn't plow back lanes. A bin truck needs room to tip the box off, and so does the truck that comes back for it. The Garage Shelf a Bin Company Won't Take

Renovation debris is mostly drywall, wood and shingles. A garage is different. It's where a house keeps its paint, its fuel, its batteries and its chemicals, which are the things a bin company turns away. On 12 November 2026 we read the published rules of several Calgary bin rental companies. Paint, aerosols, batteries, propane tanks and chemicals were refused across them. Tires, mattresses, fridges, freezers and air conditioners were allowed by some for an added fee per item. Every company sets its own list, so check yours, then walk your garage with this table:

Count the rows that apply to your garage. Each one is a separate trip in your own vehicle, on top of filling the bin, and the hazardous waste drop-off and the landfill are in different places. If paint or electronics end up inside a garbage load at a City landfill, the whole load is charged as mixed garbage, so a bin company has the same reason to turn them away.

When a Bin Is the Better Buy

A bin usually wins when most of these are true:

  • The garage is full of one kind of waste, like drywall, old cabinets or shingles from a project, not forty years of a household.
  • You've already pulled out the paint, propane, batteries and chemicals.
  • The bin can sit entirely on your own driveway, and you can park outside while it's there.
  • You're working over several weekends and want somewhere to throw things as you go.
  • You and a helper can lift a freezer, a treadmill and a workbench safely.

A crew usually wins when any of these are true:

  • Most of the job is deciding what stays: a parent's estate, two households merged into one, or boxes nobody has opened since the move. Our estate garage cleanout page explains how we handle papers and valuables.
  • The garage opens onto a lane, or the driveway is short, shared or sloped.
  • There's a date: possession, a listing, a tenant moving in. See cleanout before selling or, if it's this week, same-day cleanout.
  • It's winter and the driveway or lane is icy.
  • Nobody in the house should be carrying a freezer.
  • The volume has got ahead of the people who live there. Our hoarding garage cleanup page covers how we handle that quietly.

If you only want help deciding what to keep, with less hauling, a garage declutter is smaller than a full cleanout.

The Free Option Is Closed Until Spring

Every year the City of Calgary runs Community Cleanups, where residents bring household items that don't fit in the black cart to a City garbage truck, hosted by community associations. The 2026 cleanups ran city-wide from April 25 to September 27. The City's page now says they have wrapped up and to check back in spring 2027 for the schedule.

If your garage can wait until then, here is what they take. City trucks at the cleanups accept non-donatable furniture like mattresses, sofas and dressers, oversized household items, toilets and sinks, cabinetry, broken recreational items such as old treadmills, lumber from old fences and decks, carpets and rugs, and ceramics. They don't accept car batteries, electronics, tires, metal, barbecues, lawnmowers, small appliances, microwaves, glass, fridges and freezers, liquids, propane, paint or household chemicals. Commercial and contractor waste isn't accepted, and the hosting community association can change what's taken on the day.

That second list is close to the bin companies' refused list, and to the shelf table above. The same things that need their own trip now will still need it in May. If you're planning to wait for spring, our spring garage cleanout page covers the post-winter grit and the April rush.

Garage Bin Questions From Calgary Homeowners

How much does it cost to rent a bin in Calgary?

Advertised starting prices for Calgary roll-off bins in a 12 November 2026 search ran from $170 to $350, and at least one company listed its price as plus landfill fees. The rental is only the first line. Extra days, weight over the bin's allowance, per-item charges for mattresses, tires and fridges, and a $31.50-a-day City street use permit if the bin sits in a lane or on the street can all be added. Your own trips to drop off what the bin won't take come on top of that. The worksheet above lists every line.

What can't go in a rental bin in Calgary?

The bin companies' own lists we read on 12 November 2026 refuse paint, aerosols, batteries, propane tanks and household chemicals. Several accept tires, mattresses, fridges, freezers and air conditioners only for an added per-item fee. Each company sets its own list, so read it before you load. In a garage, the refused items are usually the paint shelf, the propane cylinders, the car batteries, the pool and lawn chemicals and the old freezer. The shelf table above shows where each one goes instead.

Do I need a permit to put a bin in the back lane in Calgary?

Yes. The City of Calgary requires a street use permit to use any City road right-of-way, which it defines as a street, sidewalk, alley, walkway, back lane or boulevard, and it lists placing a garbage bin as one of the reasons. The fee is $31.50 a day plus GST. You apply online through myID, and the City asks for at least five business days for lower-impact closures. A hoarding permit can also be required if the closure lasts more than 24 hours or affects weekday rush hours. A bin entirely on your own driveway does not use the City's road.

What items are accepted at Calgary Community Cleanups?

City garbage trucks at Community Cleanups take non-donatable furniture such as mattresses, sofas and dressers, oversized household items, toilets and sinks, cabinetry, broken recreational items like old treadmills, lumber from old fences and decks, carpets and rugs, and ceramics. They don't take car batteries, electronics, tires, metal, barbecues, lawnmowers, small appliances, microwaves, glass, fridges and freezers, liquids, propane, paint or household chemicals. The 2026 cleanups have wrapped up, and the City says to check back in spring 2027 for next year's schedule.

How do I clean out a garage full of stuff?

Sort before anything gets loaded, because Calgary charges by what's mixed together. Pull out paint, chemicals, propane and batteries first and take them to a City household hazardous waste drop-off, which is free. Keep tires and electronics apart, since the landfill takes them free when they're separate. Set aside what can be donated or sold. What's left is garbage, and that's the only part that should go in a bin or over the landfill scale. If that sort is more than a weekend's work, it's the part a crew does for you.

What is the cheapest way to get rid of garage junk in Calgary?

Doing the hauling yourself in your own vehicle, with the free streams kept separate. Household hazardous waste is free at City drop-offs, tires and electronics are free at the landfill when they're kept out of the garbage, and a residential load under 250 kg costs a flat $25 at a City landfill in 2026. That works for a garage you can clear in a few car trips. A bin or a crew starts to make sense when the volume is more than your vehicle can carry in a weekend.
