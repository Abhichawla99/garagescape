Our Chestermere Garage Organization Process

1

Walkthrough & Layout Plan

We walk your garage with you — new build or established home — and map out how you actually use it: which vehicles park where, what gear comes and goes with the seasons, and what's just been sitting.

2

Declutter & Sort

New Build in Chestermere? Plan the Garage Before You Fill It

The Blank-Box Problem

Chestermere's population has grown from just over a thousand permanent residents in the early 1990s to more than 22,000 today, and most of that growth is recent, drywalled, and moved-in within the last few years. A brand-new triple garage arrives as an empty painted box with a single builder shelf. Homeowners fill it the way everyone does — a box here, a bike there — and eighteen months later the system that should have existed from day one never got built.

Plan It at Move-In, Not Move-Out

The best time to design a Chestermere garage system is the week you get the keys, before anything has a "spot" that isn't really working. We measure the actual triple-bay footprint, plan for the boat or trailer if you have one, and install cabinetry and wall systems around real vehicles and real gear instead of retrofitting around two years of accumulated clutter.

A Triple Garage Should Feel Bigger, Not Fuller

The whole point of paying for a third bay is extra capacity. Without a plan, that capacity just becomes a bigger pile. We split the space into a parking zone, a gear zone for lake and sports equipment, and a workshop or overflow zone — so the size you're paying for actually shows up in how the garage feels to use.

A dedicated workshop wall, not a pile on the benchSlatwall or pegboard next to a real workbench keeps hand tools visible and grabbable, and leaves the third bay usable for actual projects instead of becoming a catch-all.

Lake Gear Deserves Its Own Zone

Chestermere Runs on the Lake

Chestermere Lake started as an irrigation reservoir in the early 1900s and is now the reason most people picked this city — waterskiing, wakeboarding, fishing, the Calgary Yacht Club, and the annual Water Festival all run off it. That means most garages here are storing boards, tubes, skis, life jackets, and dock gear alongside the usual household overflow, often with no system separating one from the other.

Overhead and Wall Storage for Water Toys

Wakeboards and skis go on a vertical wall rack, tubes and life jackets in a labelled bin system at grabbing height, and bulkier gear like a smaller trailer or dock hardware on an overhead platform through the off-season. The goal is a five-minute load-up on a Saturday morning, not a search through every corner of the garage.

Winter Storage, Summer Access

From fall through spring, lake gear moves up and back while winter tires, a snowblower, and ice gear move to the front. We build the swap into the system itself, so the seasonal changeover takes twenty minutes instead of an afternoon.

Labelled bins beat loose piles every timeA shelving system with clearly labelled totes for wakeboards, life jackets, and dock hardware means anyone in the house can find the right bin without a phone call.

Frequently Asked Questions About Garage Organization in Chestermere

Do you service Chestermere, or just Calgary?

Chestermere is regular territory for us, not an add-on trip. It sits right against Calgary's eastern edge, and we schedule Chestermere projects on the same routes as our east Calgary work, so there's no long-distance surcharge or special scheduling window.

My garage is a new build with nothing in it — where do we even start?

That's actually the easiest garage to organize well, because nothing has to be undone first. We design the full layout — vehicle bay, gear zone, and wall or overhead storage — around how you actually plan to use the space, so you never go through the fill-it-randomly-then-redo-it cycle that most homeowners do a year or two after moving in.

Can you build storage for a boat, wakeboards, and lake gear?

Yes — lake gear is a big part of what we organize in Chestermere. We build dedicated zones for wakeboards, tubes, skis, life jackets, and dock hardware, usually on wall racks or overhead platforms so they're out of the way all winter and grabbed in thirty seconds come May.

I have a triple garage but it's still a mess — can you help?

This is one of the most common calls we get from Chestermere's newer communities. A triple garage without a plan just means three bays of clutter instead of one. We zone the space properly — parking, gear, and workshop — so the extra square footage actually functions as extra space instead of disappearing under boxes.

Do you work on older Chestermere homes with smaller garages too?

Absolutely. Established communities like Westmere, Lakeside Greens, and Lakeview Landing often have single or double detached garages built in the 1990s and early 2000s.
