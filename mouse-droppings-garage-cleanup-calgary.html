Mouse Droppings in Your Calgary Garage: Cleanup and Clear-Out

Don't sweep it. Text us photos of where you found the droppings and we'll clear the garage the way Alberta's hantavirus guidance says to: aired out, soaked, bagged, and every box sorted into keep, wash or throw out.

It usually happens in late November. Someone goes out to the garage for the Christmas bin, lifts the lid and finds droppings on the tree skirt, or the cardboard box of lights has a chewed corner and a nest of shredded paper inside. Mice move into Calgary garages once the nights get cold, and a stack of boxes on the slab that nobody has touched since last January is exactly what they're looking for.

The instinct is to grab a broom or the shop-vac and get it over with. Don't. Deer mice carry hantavirus, and the Government of Alberta says it spreads when people breathe in particles from droppings, urine or nests that get stirred into the air. Sweeping does exactly that. The province counts 81 cases and 15 deaths in Alberta since reporting began in 1989, including one death in 2026 (alberta.ca, read November 24, 2026). It's rare, and the way to keep it rare is to not stir up the dust.

Tonight, Before Anyone Touches It

You don't need to fix anything tonight. You need to avoid making it worse, and to keep what you'll need later for a quote or an insurance call.

If you only have one dropping on one lid, you can handle it yourself with Alberta's steps (they're below). The calls we get are for the other version: droppings along the whole back wall, nesting in three or four boxes, and a garage too full to see where else they've been.

Deer Mouse, House Mouse, or a Rat?

Two kinds of mice turn up in Calgary garages, and only one of them is the usual hantavirus carrier. You can tell them apart if you see the animal. You can't tell them apart from droppings, which is why every garage gets the same treatment.

Telling Calgary garage rodents apart
What you sawHow to recognize itWhat it means for the cleanup
Deer mouseBrown or grey back with a clean white belly and white feet, large eyes and ears, tail sharply darker on top and white underneath.The usual hantavirus carrier, according to Alberta's guidance. Full soak-and-bag process.
House mouseGrey-brown all over with a slightly lighter grey belly, smaller eyes, a long tail with very little hair.Same process anyway. You'll rarely know which species left which droppings.
Only droppings, no animalSmall, pointed, about the size of a grain of rice, scattered along walls and in boxes.Treated as deer mouse droppings.
Something rat-sizedMuch bigger than a mouse, with droppings closer to the size of a raisin.Report it. Alberta has kept rats from establishing since 1950 and asks for sightings at 310-FARM (3276) or rats@gov.ab.ca, with a photo if it's safe.

That last row is where Alberta differs. In most of North America, "is it mice or rats?" is a real question in a garage. Here, a rat in a Calgary garage is a provincial matter, and the province's rat control program would rather hear about a false alarm than miss one.

Alberta's Steps, Scaled Up to a Whole Garage

The Government of Alberta's hantavirus page is written for one spot: open up, stay out 30 minutes, put on rubber gloves, soak droppings, nests and dead mice with one part bleach to nine parts water (or a household disinfectant) and wait five minutes, pick it up with paper towels, bag it, seal the bag, put it in a garbage container with a tight lid, wash your gloves before taking them off, and never sweep or vacuum. Our hoarding garage cleanup page lists them in full. A double garage full of boxes needs the same steps done in a planned order.

  1. Door up, everyone out. The overhead door and any side door open for at least 30 minutes before the crew goes in. Detached lane garages with boarded windows get longer.
  2. Work from the door inward. We soak and clear the zone nearest the door first, so there's a clean path for every bag that leaves. Nobody walks a sealed bag through an area that hasn't been soaked.
  3. Spray bottles, not a hose. The soak goes on with hand sprayers. The City of Calgary's Stage 4 outdoor water restrictions, in effect since November 4, 2026, rule out hosing anything down, and a hose would spread the mess across the slab anyway.
  4. Bag whole, double-bag, seal. Nested boxes go into heavy bags without being emptied, then into a second bag.
  5. Shelves, then the slab edge. Once the contents are out, every shelf and the strip of floor along the walls gets soaked and wiped, because that's where mice travel.
  6. Gloves washed before they come off. Then hands. Every time a crew member leaves the garage.

One Calgary problem the province's page doesn't cover: cold. A one-to-nine bleach mix is mostly water, and on a minus 20 morning it starts to freeze on a concrete slab in an unheated detached garage. If your garage is unheated and a cold snap lands on the booked day, we'll move the soak to a milder day rather than wipe ice around. Attached garages that stay above freezing aren't affected.

If the infestation is heavy and the garage has little airflow, Alberta says to call Health Link at 811 about precautions first. We'll make that call with you before we book, not after.

Where the Sealed Bags Go in Calgary

For a few dead mice, the City of Calgary's answer is simple: double-bag them and put them in your black cart as garbage. Two things trip people up. Black carts are collected every two weeks, and the cart has to be out at the curb or lane by 7 a.m. on collection day, because City collectors don't go onto private property. A cart left inside the garage doesn't get emptied.

A garage full of chewed boxes doesn't fit in a black cart, and holding sealed bags in the garage for two weeks gives mice a reason to come back. So on a full cleanup the bags go with us, same day, to a City landfill as garbage. The City charges $25 for a load under 250 kg and charges by weight above that. Our garage junk removal cost page explains how that adds up on a mixed load. Landfills are closed Sundays, so a Saturday cleanup finishes its dump run that afternoon.

Garages with mice tend to have old pest products in them too: half-used bait packs, a box of rodenticide pellets, ant powder from years ago. Those don't go in the garbage load. They go with any paint and fuel to a City household hazardous waste drop-off at a participating fire station.

What Changes the Quote

We don't have a flat price for mouse cleanups, because two garages with "mice" can be a fifteen-minute job or a full day. These are the things that move it:

  • How far the droppings have spread. One shelf and a corner is quick. Droppings in every box and along every wall means the whole garage gets the soak-and-bag treatment.
  • How many boxes you want opened. Bagging a nested box whole takes a minute. Opening it on a soaked surface and checking each item takes much longer, and is worth it for the box of photo albums.
  • How much goes to the landfill. Contaminated cardboard, cushions and seed bags add weight, and the City charges by weight above 250 kg.
  • Whether it's part of a bigger cleanout. If you were already planning a full garage cleanout, a downsizing move or an estate cleanout, the mouse work folds into that job.
  • Heated or not. An unheated detached garage in January may need a second visit for the soak if the first day is too cold.

Before you call us, check one thing: your home insurance. Many policies exclude damage caused by rodents or vermin, and some don't. Questions About Mice in a Calgary Garage

What should I do if I find mouse droppings in my garage?

Leave it alone for now. Don't sweep, shop-vac or carry the box inside. Close the door between the garage and the house, keep children and pets out, and take photos of where the droppings are. When you or a crew clean it, follow the Government of Alberta's steps: air the garage out for at least 30 minutes, wear rubber gloves, soak droppings, nests and dead mice with one part bleach to nine parts water for five minutes, pick it up with paper towels, bag and seal it, and never sweep or vacuum.

Do exterminators clean up mouse droppings?

Some Calgary pest control companies offer dropping cleanup for a defined area, and most focus on trapping and sealing entry points. Few will empty a full garage, open or bag every box, haul the contaminated load to the landfill and wipe the shelving. That is the part we do. The usual order is trapping first, then the clear-out, then sealing, because the walls and slab edge are only reachable once the garage is empty.

How long do mouse droppings stay dangerous?

Alberta's guidance doesn't give an age after which droppings are safe, so treat old and fresh droppings the same way: soak first, never sweep or vacuum. Fresh droppings are dark and soft and tell you mice are still active, which matters for whether trapping has to happen before the cleanup.

Can you get rid of mice in a garage for good?

You can make the garage much less attractive. Mice come for nesting material and food: cardboard boxes on the slab, birdseed, grass seed and pet food in bags, and fabric left in piles. Once those are gone and a pest control company or handyman has fixed the gaps, such as a worn bottom seal on the overhead door or a missing door sweep, mice have far less reason to stay. A garage door opens many times a day, so no garage is sealed forever.

Does home insurance cover mouse damage in a garage?

It depends on your policy. Many home policies exclude damage caused by rodents or vermin, and some don't. Photograph everything before it's cleaned or thrown out, then read the exclusions section or call your broker. We can hold the cleanup until you've made that call.

Was that a rat in my Calgary garage?

Almost certainly not. Alberta has run a rat control program since 1950 and keeps rats from becoming established. If you believe you saw a rat, the province asks you to report it to 310-FARM (3276) or rats@gov.ab.ca, with the location and a photo if it's safe to take one.

Do I need to be home while you clean the garage?

Not for the whole job. We need you, or someone you trust, for the first fifteen minutes to point out the boxes that matter, and to be reachable by phone. Boxes you haven't marked get bagged whole if mice have been in them, rather than emptied on the floor.

How much does mouse cleanup in a garage cost?

It depends on how far the droppings have spread, how many boxes you want opened rather than bagged whole, and how much goes to the landfill.
