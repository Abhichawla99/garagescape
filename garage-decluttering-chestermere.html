With a powered lake at the doorstep, many Chestermere garages have to share space with a boat, a trailer, or a smaller RV for part of the year. Chestermere sits on open prairie with far less tree cover than inner-city Calgary, so wind-driven dust blows in every time the door lifts and coats anything left on the floor. Two sets of tires, a snowblower, shovels, ice melt, and block-heater cords are standard here. We build a defined off-season zone so the fall and spring tire swap takes minutes instead of an afternoon of shuffling boxes around the boat.
