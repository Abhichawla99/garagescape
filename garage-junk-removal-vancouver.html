The Difference Between Hauling and Removing

Vancouver has good volume-based junk haulers, and for a pre-sorted pile of genuine garbage they're the right call — we'll tell you so on the phone. Their model is simple: truck space costs money, everything in the truck is billable, off it goes.

The problem is what a real garage actually contains. When we clear a typical Vancouver double garage, the load breaks down roughly like this:

Where the contents of a typical unsorted Vancouver garage actually belong.

~25–35%Paint, oil, batteries, electronics, tires, propane, bulbsBC stewardship depotsFree
~15–25%Usable furniture, tools, bikes, sporting goodsCharities and reuse organisationsFree
~10%Scrap metal, clean wood, cardboardRecycling / reduced-rate streamsLow
~5%Returnable containersBottle depotPays you
~30–40%Genuine garbage, broken goods, mattressesTransfer station / landfillTipping fees

In a volume truck, all five rows bill the same. Sorted, only the last row costs real money — and Metro Vancouver tipping fees being what they are in 2026, that difference is not small. Our full fee breakdown is on the Vancouver garage cleanout page.

The hazmat point most haulers skip

Household hazardous waste — paint, solvents, gasoline, propane, pesticides — is banned from Metro Vancouver garbage loads, and loads found containing it attract surcharges. A hauler who cheerfully throws your paint cans in with the couch is creating a problem billed to somebody, eventually. We route it to the free depots it legally belongs at, which costs you nothing and is the entire point of the stewardship programs.

What We Take From Vancouver Garages

  • Furniture and appliances — usable pieces donated, the rest recycled or tipped
  • Renovation leftovers — lumber, tile, fixtures; drywall separated because it's the most expensive item by weight
  • Old sports and outdoor gear — bikes especially have strong reuse channels in Vancouver
  • Electronics, tools and small appliances — free stewardship returns
  • Paint, oil, antifreeze, batteries, propane — routed to designated depots at no charge
  • Water-damaged and mouldy contents — bagged and handled properly, common after wet winters
  • Rodent-contaminated material — with appropriate PPE and disposal
  • General accumulated junk — the boxes nobody has opened since the last move

The two things that stop a job: suspected asbestos (pre-1990 vinyl flooring, pipe wrap, some cement board — testing first, certified abatement if positive), and chemical quantities beyond household scale. We tell you immediately rather than quietly leaving them behind.

Junk Removal, Cleanout or Decluttering?

Three services, one honest sorting rule:

  • You know what's garbage and it's already in a pile → junk removal. This page. From $399, or cheaper with a volume hauler if it's truly pre-sorted.
  • The whole garage needs to be emptied — estate, moving, pre-renovation → garage cleanout. Everything comes out, everything gets routed.
  • The problem is deciding what stays → garage decluttering. Sorting with you, not for you — and the space comes back organised.

Most people who call about "junk removal" turn out to want the second or third. That's fine — the walkthrough quote is free either way, and pricing the right service beats underpricing the wrong one.

Garage Junk Removal Vancouver — FAQs

How much does garage junk removal cost in Vancouver?

From $399 with us, including sorting, loading, hauling and donation routing, plus landfill disposal at cost. Volume haulers charge $150–$650 by truck space. A pre-sorted garbage pile favours the volume hauler and we'll say so; a typical unsorted garage — where a third disposes free — usually comes out ahead sorted.

What won't you take?

We route rather than refuse most things. The genuine stops: suspected asbestos material, which needs testing and certified abatement, and chemical quantities beyond household scale. Everything else — paint, propane, batteries, electronics — has a legal channel and we take it there.

Is it cheaper if I sort first myself?

Yes, and then you may not need us at all — a volume hauler takes a pre-sorted garbage pile cheaply. Our service exists for the common case: nothing is sorted, decisions haven't been made, and half the value is making the keep-donate-dispose calls with you.

Do you take paint, propane and car batteries?

Yes, and they ride free — Product Care for paint, BC Used Oil for oil and antifreeze, stewardship depots for batteries and electronics, designated facilities for propane. These are banned from Metro Vancouver garbage loads anyway; mixing them in exposes you to surcharges.

Can you come same-day?

Often, depending on the day and where you are — deadline situations get priority. Late-day pickups may be tipped the next morning because disposal facilities keep fixed hours; that doesn't change your price.

What happens to it all afterwards?

Reuse first, stewardship second, recycling third, landfill last. You get the disposal receipts and a summary of what was donated, recycled and landfilled with the invoice.
