Estate Cleanout in Cochrane, Starting With the Garage

For the executor, or the son or daughter driving out from Calgary on weekends, with a parent's Cochrane house to settle. We sort the garage and shop with you, send each thing to the place in Cochrane or Calgary that will actually take it, keep the empty house from drawing wildlife, and hand over a swept garage.

Clearing an estate in Cochrane has one problem a Calgary estate doesn't. The town's Eco Centre is built for small household drop-offs, and it turns away several things every estate garage has: the old couch, the spare mattress, the treadmill, the leftover drywall. Those have to go down the highway to a City of Calgary landfill. Meanwhile the Eco Centre caps paint and chemicals per household per week, so a garage with decades of product in it can't be emptied in one Saturday trip.

This page is built around those two rules, plus the third thing that matters in a foothills town: an empty house with a full garage is an easy meal for bears. Eco Centre, Donation or Calgary: Where Each Load Goes

The Cochrane Eco Centre is at 50 Griffin Industrial Point. It's self-sort, closed Sundays, Mondays and statutory holidays, and the Town describes it as a place for small amounts of recyclables and household waste. For large loads, the Town's own page points people to City of Calgary landfills and private recyclers. Here is how the contents of a typical estate garage split between the two, from the Town's page as of 26 November 2026:

Fees and limits change, so check the Town's page or call the Eco Centre at 403-851-2277 before a trip. The City of Calgary landfill on the northwest side is Spyhill, entered from 69 Street NW.

What this means for the job: if we sort before we load, most of an estate garage stays in Cochrane. Appliances, e-waste, metal and bagged garbage go to Griffin Industrial Point, usable goods go to local charities, and only the furniture, mattresses, gym equipment and building leftovers make the Calgary run. If the garage has none of those, there may be no Calgary trip at all. Loaded unsorted, the same garage has nowhere to go in Cochrane, and the whole lot ends up on the Calgary run.

Donating in Cochrane

Cochrane Home Treasures is a non-profit second-hand store in town that sells household goods, furniture and small appliances. The Neighbour to Neighbour Thrift Shop supports the Cochrane Food Bank, and its manager has asked people to bring donations only during open hours instead of leaving them outside. Call either before you bring big pieces. Goodwill's Alberta site says furniture only goes to its Impact Centres in Calgary and Edmonton.

The Weekly Limits, Worked Through One Garage

The Eco Centre's limits for paint, hazardous waste and oil are per household, per week. That's fine for a normal clear-out. An estate garage is different, because a parent who lived in the same house for thirty years kept every can. Here's an example of how the limits play out. The quantities below are an illustration of a common estate garage, not a real client's.

In that example the hazardous shelf alone needs three separate weeks of visits, and the Eco Centre is only open Tuesday to Saturday. If the family starts the week before possession, it won't fit. So we pull the hazardous products out on the first day, before any hauling, and box them by category in the original containers. Then the trips can start that same week while the rest of the garage is still being sorted. Ask the attendants how they count an estate as a household on the first trip, and we'll plan the remaining trips around their answer.

Propane cylinders and unlabelled jars

Ask the Eco Centre about barbecue propane cylinders before the trip, and never puncture or crush one. Jars and coffee tins of unknown liquid stay closed and unmixed, and they go in as they are so the staff can decide.

An Empty House With a Full Garage, in Bear Country

Black bears come into Cochrane neighbourhoods looking for food, and one found in Fireside was destroyed (Cochrane Now). A house that sits empty while an estate is settled is a bigger risk than a lived-in one, because nobody is there to notice a smell or bring the carts in. The Town's wildlife page sets the basic rules. Here is how they apply to an estate garage:

  • The garage freezer comes first. If the power has been off or the freezer is failing, a chest full of meat is the strongest attractant on the property. We empty it on the first day.
  • Birdseed, pet food and grass seed. Older owners often kept 20 kg bags in the garage. Those go out sealed with the first load. The Town asks residents to take bird feeders down from April 1 to November 30, so check the yard too.
  • The barbecue. The Town says to clean it after use and store it indoors. If it's staying with the house for the sale, we scrape the grease tray and move it into the garage.
  • Carts on collection day. Carts go out the morning of collection, not the night before. At an empty house, arrange with a neighbour or the realtor who puts them out and brings them back. While we're loading, we keep garbage bags in the truck or the closed garage, not on the driveway.
  • Gaps and crawl spaces. The Town recommends sealing them. We note any open vents or chewed corners in the garage on the walk-through so the family can have them fixed before the listing.

Inside Town Limits or Out in Rocky View

A lot of Cochrane estates aren't in town. Parents who farmed or kept horses west and north of Cochrane often lived on acreages in Rocky View County, with a shop, a barn or a quonset as well as the garage. Two things change.

First, Rocky View County's site says the Cochrane Eco Centre can be used by County residents and lists Cochrane as a year-round household hazardous waste site. County transfer sites take only County residents' material. Second, outbuildings mean more volume, more metal and often more fuel and ammunition, so the walk-through takes longer and the job usually runs over several days. Our acreage garage and shop cleanout page covers mice in outbuildings, grain bags, twine and wire, and fuel storage in detail.

In town, the estate houses we see are spread across older streets like Glenbow and West Terrace and newer areas like Sunset Ridge, Fireside and GlenEagles. A front-drive double garage in a newer area usually means a clear driveway to load from. An older house with a detached garage or a tight lane means more carrying, and we price that in.

If Something Valuable or Dangerous Turns Up

Cash, jewellery, coins, watches and paperwork go into a set-aside box the moment they turn up and are handed to the personal representative, not to whoever happens to be on site. We keep all paperwork, including old tax returns, vehicle registrations and letters, because executors often need records that were stored in the garage.

We don't touch firearms or ammunition. Cochrane is policed by the RCMP, so the personal representative can call the Cochrane detachment's non-emergency line and ask how they want a surrender handled. For keeping or selling a firearm to a licensed owner, the RCMP Canadian Firearms Program is at 1-800-731-4000. Don't put a firearm in your car and drive it anywhere without asking first.

What Makes a Cochrane Quote Bigger or Smaller

We quote after a free walk-through. These are the things that move the number, roughly in order of how much they matter:

  • How much can only go to Calgary. Furniture that can't be donated, mattresses, gym equipment and renovation leftovers each mean a load down the highway and landfill fees.
  • Appliance fees. $40 for each fridge or freezer and $30 for each stove or washer at the Eco Centre, passed through at cost on the invoice with the receipt.
  • Total volume. A packed double garage is a different job from a half-full single.
  • Outbuildings. A shop, barn or second garage on an acreage is quoted as its own phase.
  • How many sorting days the family needs. One walk-through then a clear-out is quicker than three weekend visits, and either is fine.
  • Access. Lane garages, stairs to a loft, or a long acreage driveway in winter.

If the estate would rather approve one part at a time, we quote in phases: garage, then shop, then the floor wash once the slab is empty. For the floor itself, see garage floor cleaning and oil stain removal, both of which we do in Cochrane too.

Questions From Cochrane Families

How much does an estate cleanout cost in Cochrane?

It depends on how much has to leave the property, how much of it can only go to a Calgary landfill, how many appliances carry an Eco Centre fee, whether there is a shop or outbuilding as well as the garage, and how many separate days the family needs for sorting. We quote after a free walk-through, and we can quote in phases if the estate wants to approve one part at a time.

Does the Cochrane Eco Centre take furniture or mattresses?

No. The Town of Cochrane's Eco Centre page says furniture should be donated or taken to a Calgary landfill, and that mattresses and box springs, fitness equipment and water softeners go to a Calgary landfill. Construction waste is not accepted. Metal furniture can go in the Eco Centre's mixed metal bin.

Where do I take old paint and chemicals from a Cochrane garage?

The Cochrane Eco Centre at 50 Griffin Industrial Point. Its page lists limits per household per week: up to 30 litres of paint and aerosols, up to 10 litres of other household hazardous waste, and up to 40 litres of oil. A garage with a lot of old product can take several weekly trips, so start early. Check cochrane.ca for current hours; it is closed Sundays, Mondays and statutory holidays.

Can Rocky View County residents use the Cochrane Eco Centre?

Rocky View County's website says the Cochrane site is operated by a neighbouring municipality but can be used by County residents, and it lists Cochrane as a year-round household hazardous waste site. For some items, such as styrofoam, the Town's page says attendants may ask for proof of residency.

Where can I donate furniture and household items in Cochrane?

Cochrane Home Treasures is a non-profit second-hand store in town, and the Neighbour to Neighbour Thrift Shop supports the Cochrane Food Bank. Call ahead about large pieces and drop-off hours, and don't leave items outside when they're closed. Goodwill's Alberta site says it takes furniture only at its Impact Centres in Calgary and Edmonton.

Do I need to be in Cochrane for the cleanout?

No. One named contact approves decisions, usually the personal representative. We text photos of anything uncertain and hold set-aside items until that person answers. Many families come out from Calgary for the first walk-through and leave the rest to us.

Who pays for an estate cleanout in Alberta?

It's an estate expense. The personal representative usually pays, either personally or from the estate account once it's open, and is reimbursed from the estate. Keep our invoice and every Eco Centre and landfill receipt for the estate accounts.

What happens if you find a firearm in a Cochrane garage?

We stop work in that area, leave it where it is and tell you. We don't handle or transport firearms or ammunition. Cochrane is policed by the RCMP, so the personal representative should call the Cochrane detachment's non-emergency line to arrange a surrender, or the RCMP Canadian Firearms Program at 1-800-731-4000 about transferring it to a licensed owner.

Are you insured for estate cleanouts?

Yes. GarageScape carries $2 million in liability insurance. If the estate lawyer or a condo board wants proof before we start, ask on the first call.
