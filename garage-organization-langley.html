Which Garage Organization System Goes on Which Wall

People search for "garage organization systems" expecting one product. A working garage usually has three or four, each doing one job on the wall that suits it. We specify them after we measure, and we quote the hardware at cost to a budget you set.

Willoughby Townhouse and Tandem Garages

Willoughby is newer than Walnut Grove. The Township moved its municipal hall there in 2006, and the blocks around 208 Street and 80 Avenue are some of the densest in Langley. In a townhome or a small-lot house, the garage is often the only storage the home has, and it is narrower than the floor plan suggested.

In a tandem garage the back half becomes a storage room within months, and the second car moves to the street. We treat the two halves as separate spaces. The front stall stays clear for one vehicle. The back half gets shelving on both side walls with a walkway down the middle wide enough to carry a bin through, so nothing has to come out before you can reach what you need.

The car goes back inAn example layout, not a photo of a Langley client's garage. We mark the parking envelope first and build storage around it, which is the whole point in a narrow Willoughby garage.

Strata garages need a bylaw check first

Many strata corporations restrict what an owner can fasten to a wall shared with a neighbour, and some require a garage to stay available for parking. Send us your bylaws with the quote request. Where drilling is restricted we use freestanding shelving that is braced and levelled, and we keep the stall clear.

Walnut Grove and Murrayville Doubles

Walnut Grove filled in after the Trans-Canada Highway opened in 1964, and it is now one of Langley's established suburbs. A double garage that one family has used for twenty years holds the record of that time: children's bikes in three sizes, a second fridge, paint from every room, camping gear, and boxes that came with the move and were never opened.

A new system installed on top of all that only organizes things you do not want. We sort first. Each item is kept, donated, recycled or disposed of, and you make every call. Once the volume is honest, a standard double takes wall track down one side, shelving across the back and an overhead rack above the hoods. Both cars fit, and you can reach the house door without turning sideways.

If the garage needs clearing more than it needs shelving, start with garage decluttering and add the storage system once the floor is visible.

Older Garages in Brookswood and Fernridge

Brookswood sits south of the City of Langley, with its village centre at 200 Street and 40 Avenue. It is an established area with spacious yards, and many of its homes have not been updated in years. Garages here can be detached, sometimes with bare stud walls, and a garage shaded by trees stays damp through the winter.

Dry the wall before you shelve itAn example layout, not a photo of a Langley client's garage. A stained wall base gets cleaned and dried first. New storage then stands off the wall and off the slab so air can move behind it.

In these garages we check the wall before we hang anything on it. Bare studs take wall track well. Old panelling over unknown framing does not, and we find the studs or use freestanding shelving. If the base of the wall shows staining or mould, that gets treated first. Our page on mould in a garage explains what we look for.

Storing Things Through a Langley Winter

Environment Canada's 1991 to 2020 normals for Abbotsford Airport, the station commonly used for Langley, record 1,504.6 mm of precipitation a year. November is the wettest month at 233.1 mm. An unheated garage spends that month at close to outdoor humidity, and the concrete slab stays cold and slightly damp until spring.

Cardboard is the first thing to failAn example layout, not a photo of a Langley client's garage. Boxes on a slab wick moisture from below. The contents follow: books, photos, fabric and anything with a paper label.

Three rules cover most of the risk. Contents go into sealed plastic bins. The lowest shelf sits off the floor so a wet slab or a leaking door seal never reaches it. Anything mounted on an outside wall gets an air gap behind it. Steel shelving in a damp garage should be galvanized or powder coated, and we specify it that way.

Acreage Shops and Horse Properties

The Township calls itself the Horse Capital of BC, and its equine industry is valued at more than $60 million a year. On an acreage, the garage and the shop carry things a suburban garage never sees: saddles and tack, feed bins, fencing wire and posts, a ride-on mower, a quad and the parts for all of them.

We plan these buildings by weight and by frequency of use. Machinery gets a fixed parking position with a clear path to the door. Feed goes into sealed containers off the floor, away from fuel and chemicals. Tack hangs on racks in the driest part of the building. Hardware and parts go into labelled small-parts storage beside the bench. A large shop is quoted on site and usually done in phases, one zone per visit, so the building stays usable while the work is underway.

Where the Rest Goes

Every organizing job produces a pile that is leaving. If decluttering is part of your quote, we take it. If you are doing that part yourself, these are the Langley options.

  • Langley Recycling and Waste Centre. Metro Vancouver's site at 1070 272 Street in Aldergrove takes household garbage and recycling. Used drywall is accepted only when it is bagged to the centre's rules.
  • Township large-item pickup. Curbside garbage customers in the Township can book pickups for bulky items. Televisions, tires, propane tanks, car parts and hazardous waste are excluded. The current limits are on tol.ca.
  • Household hazardous waste. The Township and the City of Langley have run a joint drop-off event for paint, solvents, motor oil, antifreeze and batteries, with proof of Langley residency. Paint can also go to depots that accept it year round. Check tol.ca/hhw for dates.

Garage Organization Langley: Frequently Asked Questions

How much do garage organization systems cost in Langley?

Design and installation starts at $649 for a single garage, $999 for a standard double and $1,349 for a triple or oversized garage. Storage hardware is separate and quoted at cost to a budget you set, typically $300 to $900 for a single garage and $700 to $3,000 for a triple. Acreage shops and outbuildings are quoted on site once we have seen the volume.

Which garage organization system is best for a Langley garage?

It depends on the wall and on what you store. Wall track suits bikes, garden tools and sports gear that change with the season. Steel shelving suits sealed bins. Overhead racks suit things you touch twice a year, such as camping gear and holiday boxes. Closed cabinets suit paint, chemicals and tools you want behind a door. Most Langley garages end up with two or three of these, each on the wall where it fits, and we choose them after measuring, with no single brand to sell.

Do you organize townhouse and tandem garages in Willoughby?

Yes. In a tandem garage we keep the front stall clear for the car and put the storage on the walls of the back half, with a walkway to the house door. Strata bylaws often limit what you can fix to a shared wall, so we read your bylaws before we design and use freestanding shelving where drilling is restricted.

Do you organize acreage shops and outbuildings in Langley?

Yes. Most of the Township's land sits in the Agricultural Land Reserve, and a detached shop on an acreage holds far more than a house garage: machinery, fencing supplies, feed and tack, and tools collected over decades. We assess on site, fix a load-rated position for the heavy equipment first and build the storage around it. Large shops are phased over more than one visit.

Will boxes stored in my Langley garage survive the winter?

Cardboard on a concrete floor usually does not. Environment Canada's 1991 to 2020 normals for Abbotsford Airport, the station commonly used for Langley, show about 1,505 mm of precipitation a year and 233 mm in November alone. A slab draws that moisture up and cardboard wicks it. We move contents into sealed bins and set the lowest shelf off the floor.

What happens to the things I decide not to keep?

If you add decluttering to the job, we sort everything into keep, donate, recycle and dispose before any shelving goes up, and the disposal run is part of that quote. If you prefer to do it yourself, Metro Vancouver's Langley Recycling and Waste Centre is at 1070 272 Street in Aldergrove, and Township curbside customers can book large-item pickups through the Township. Check tol.ca for the current limits and the list of items it will not take.

Which parts of Langley do you serve?

The whole Township: Willoughby, Willowbrook, Walnut Grove, Fort Langley, Murrayville, Milner, Brookswood, Fernridge and Aldergrove, plus the City of Langley. We also work next door in Clayton and Cloverdale. If you are near a boundary, ask.
