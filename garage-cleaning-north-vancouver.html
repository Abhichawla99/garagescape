Why North Shore Garages Are the Wettest in the Region

Three things stack up here, and together they make North Vancouver a genuinely different job from a garage ten minutes south across the bridge.

Orographic rainfall. Moist Pacific air moving inland is forced upward by the North Shore mountains, cools, and drops its moisture on the way. The result is that the upper slopes of North Vancouver receive markedly more precipitation than downtown Vancouver does — and the higher up you live, the bigger the gap.

Dense conifer canopy. The tree cover that makes the North Shore beautiful also keeps garage walls and aprons permanently shaded, holds moisture against structures, and drops needles and organic debris year-round. A north-facing wall under mature cedar effectively never dries out.

Slope. North Vancouver is built on a hillside, so under-house and tuck-under garages are the norm rather than the exception. That has two consequences: the garage shares air with the living space above it, and the uphill wall is holding back a saturated slope.

The uphill wall problem

This is the single most common structural issue we find in North Vancouver. A tuck-under garage cut into a slope has one wall with wet ground behind it and, frequently, drainage that was adequate in 1975 and isn't now. Water enters at the wall-slab junction, and because that wall is usually the darkest and least visited part of the garage, the mould gets well established before anyone notices. We locate it, treat it, and tell you plainly what the drainage would need — whether or not that's work we do.

Moss, and why it matters more than it looks

Moss and green algae along the bottom of the garage door, in the door track and across the apron are close to inevitable on the North Shore. Beyond looking neglected, moss holds water directly against the structure. On a wooden garage door that rots the bottom rail; on the apron it keeps the slab edge permanently saturated. We remove it and treat the surface so it doesn't re-establish over a single wet season.

The gear problem

North Vancouver garages carry more outdoor equipment than anywhere else we work. Fromme and Seymour are minutes away, Grouse is up the road, Deep Cove is a paddling hub. A single Lynn Valley or Blueridge garage routinely holds four or five mountain bikes plus a spare frame, full ski and snowboard kit for the family, paddleboards, a kayak, wetsuits and both car-camping and backcountry gear. Storage design that ignores that fails within a month, and a bike parked on a damp North Shore slab corrodes its drivetrain from below.

Bikes never touch the floor here

A mountain bike drivetrain is expensive, and a damp concrete slab corrodes chain, cassette and bottom bracket from underneath. On the North Shore we treat floor bike storage as a non-starter — daily riders go on vertical wall hooks at accessible height, seasonal bikes on ceiling hoists. Security is a live consideration too: Vancouver has the highest bike theft rate per capita of any major Canadian city, so we keep racks out of sightlines from windows and open doors.

Garage Types We Work On in North Vancouver

Under-house and tuck-under garage

The default across most of North Vancouver. Cut into the slope, one wall holding back wet ground, shared air with the living space above. Sanitisation matters more here because garage air isn't isolated, and the uphill wall is where we look first.

Detached lane and yard garage

Lower Lonsdale, Grand Boulevard, Central Lonsdale and the older parts of Lynn Valley. Frequently pre-war, unheated, no power or water, and the worst moss exposure in the region.

Deep Cove and waterfront-adjacent garages

Tight lots, steep access, heavy paddle and water-sports gear loads. Wetsuits, boards and kayaks that come home wet, which in this climate becomes a mould source within days if they go straight into a closed garage.

Newer Edgemont, Blueridge and Seymour builds

Larger attached double garages, high gear volume, better construction. These are usually organisation jobs rather than remediation — the problem is capacity and layout, not damage.

Disposal & Drive Times from North Vancouver

The North Shore Transfer Station on Riverside Drive in North Vancouver is the closest facility for most jobs here, which keeps disposal runs short and drive time off your invoice. Larger or non-divertible loads go to the Vancouver Landfill in Delta, which is a genuinely long round trip from the North Shore — a real cost we're transparent about rather than burying in the price.

That distance is exactly why sorting properly matters more here than almost anywhere. Every item routed to free stewardship disposal, reuse or donation is one that doesn't ride to Delta. Paint, motor oil, antifreeze, electronics, batteries and tyres are all accepted free at designated BC stewardship depots, and bikes — of which North Vancouver garages have plenty — have strong local reuse channels.

Confirm current facility hours and rates before hauling your own load. Full 2026 Metro Vancouver fee detail is on our garage cleanout page.

North Vancouver Garage Services — FAQs

Does North Vancouver really get more rain than Vancouver?

Yes, and by a wide margin on the upper slopes. Moist Pacific air is forced upward by the North Shore mountains, cools and drops its moisture — orographic lift. The higher up you live, the bigger the difference. Combined with dense conifer canopy that keeps walls permanently shaded, it makes North Shore garages the wettest we work on anywhere in Metro Vancouver.

Why is there mould on only one wall of my garage?

If you have a tuck-under or under-house garage, it's almost certainly the uphill wall. That wall has wet ground behind it and drainage that may have been adequate decades ago and isn't now. Water enters at the wall-slab junction, and because it's usually the darkest and least visited part of the garage, growth gets well established before anyone notices. We locate it, treat it, and write up what the drainage would actually need.

How do you deal with moss on the garage door and apron?

Removal plus a residual treatment so it doesn't re-establish over one wet season. Moss matters more than it looks: it holds water directly against the structure, which rots the bottom rail of a wooden garage door and keeps the slab edge permanently saturated. If the door bottom has already gone soft we'll tell you, because cleaning it won't fix that.

Can you organise all our bikes and ski gear?

That's half of what we do in North Vancouver. A Lynn Valley or Blueridge garage routinely holds four or five bikes, full family ski and snowboard kit, paddleboards, a kayak, wetsuits and camping gear for two different kinds of trip. We use vertical wall hooks for daily riders, ceiling hoists for seasonal bikes, wall racks for boards and skis, and hung storage for wetsuits — nothing folded and nothing on the concrete.

How much does garage cleaning cost in North Vancouver?

A single-car garage clean starts at $249 and a standard double at $349. Heavy moss and mould treatment typically adds $80 to $200, which is more common here than elsewhere. Full declutter and organise runs $749 to $1,100; cleanouts start at $399 plus disposal at cost. We're transparent that the run to the Vancouver Landfill in Delta is long from the North Shore, which is exactly why we sort hard before hauling.

Do you cover both the City and District of North Vancouver?

Yes — Lynn Valley, Deep Cove, Lower and Central Lonsdale, Edgemont, Blueridge, Seymour, Grand Boulevard, Capilano, Dollarton and everywhere between, in both the City and the District. We also serve West Vancouver, so if you're near the boundary just ask.
