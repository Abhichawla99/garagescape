Garage and Shop Organization in Priddis, Alberta

Priddis is a Foothills County hamlet about 10 kilometres west of Calgary's city limits, just southwest of where the Cowboy Trail (Highway 22) meets Highway 22X. It takes its name from Charles Priddis, who homesteaded along Fish Creek in 1886, and it sits at about 1,160 metres in the foothills. Around the hamlet are acreages, horse properties, the estate homes at Priddis Greens and the University of Calgary's Rothney Astrophysical Observatory. Garages out here are big, often a triple or quad attached plus a heated shop, and they hold more than a city garage ever does: tractors, quads, trailers, feed, tack, golf bags and a winter's worth of fuel. We sort all of it, give each building one job, and keep anything wildlife would want sealed away.

Four Kinds of Priddis Garage

Triple and Quad Attached Garages on Acreages

Recent Priddis acreage listings describe two triple-car garages on a single gated property, and a four-car, three-door attached garage with in-floor heat and 13-foot ceilings built for vehicle lifts. Space is rarely the problem in garages like these. The problem is that every bay slowly turns into general storage. We assign each bay one job (daily vehicles, the weekend car on a lift, a gear and golf bay, or a clean workshop), then move bins, ladders and seasonal items onto wall rails and overhead racks that stay clear of the lift's travel and the door tracks.

Heated Detached Shops

One recent Priddis listing includes a heated, fully serviced 45 by 36 foot shop whose centre bay fits a 40-foot-plus Class A motorhome. A building that size needs zones or it becomes a second, bigger junk room. We split it into a vehicle and trailer side, a workbench wall with the tools you use every week, closed cabinets for the rest, a fuel and chemicals corner well away from the heater and the bench, and a parts and attachments area for the tractor.

Forested Lots With an Oversized Double and a Shed

Not every Priddis property is a ranch. Treed parcels of three to four acres with an oversized double garage and a separate shed are common too. Here we decide what lives in the garage (vehicles, the everyday tools, the boot and dog zone) and what moves to the shed (yard tools, firewood gear, the mower), so the garage stays parkable all winter.

Priddis Greens Estate Homes

Homes around the golf course have attached garages closer to a city layout, usually a double or triple with a finished interior. The jobs here look like our work in Heritage Pointe: cabinets that match the house, a golf station, a clean floor, and a third bay that stops collecting overflow.

Wildlife-Proof Storage for a Foothills Acreage

What Priddis Has Already Seen

CBC Calgary reported Priddis residents' concern over cougar attacks on pets, including a miniature horse killed on one property and a puppy attacked on a neighbour's porch. The local county councillor cited six reported incidents at the time, and Alberta Fish and Wildlife said cougar incidents were becoming more common across the province. Black bears turn up in the area as well. None of this is a reason to panic, but it is a reason to stop storing food smells in a building with a door that stays open on summer afternoons.

Follow the BearSmart List

Alberta's BearSmart guide for residents names garbage, pet food, bird seed, barbecues, grain and livestock feed as attractants. It says to keep garbage in a bear-resistant container or building, keep pet food inside, and clean the barbecue after every use and store it in the garage. We turn that list into a layout: a sealed steel or heavy plastic bin for each feed and seed, one shelf for the pet food and treats, garbage and recycling in lidded carts against the wall farthest from the overhead door, and the barbecue parked in its own marked spot once it has cooled.

Feed Rooms That Stay Feed Rooms

On horse properties, bagged feed and supplements often creep from the barn into the garage during winter so nobody has to walk out in the dark. If that is how your household works, we set up a small, sealed feed station by the house door with bins sized to the bags you buy. If it isn't, we move it all back to the barn and give the tack room shelving so it fits.

Tractors, Quads, Trailers and the Shop Wall

Every Machine Gets a Floor Bay

On an acreage the snow plan is your own: a compact tractor with a blade or blower, a quad with a plow, or a walk-behind snowblower for the paths. Each machine gets a marked bay near a door, with its attachments on wall brackets or a rolling cart beside it. The summer equipment (mower, rototiller, the trailer for brush) takes the same bays from May to October.

Lift Bays Need Clear Ceilings

A 13-foot ceiling is an invitation to hang storage overhead, and a vehicle lift needs the same space. We map the lift's full rise and the door opener's travel before we install any overhead rack, then put long-term bins over the bays that never lift.

Fuel, Oil and Chemicals in One Place

Rural properties keep more gasoline, diesel, two-stroke mix, oil, antifreeze, fence-post stain and weed killer than a city lot. We group them on a metal shelf or in a cabinet in the detached shop where possible, low and away from the heater and the house door, label every container, and take old and unlabelled product for proper disposal.

Our Priddis Garage Organization Process

1

Photo Quote or Walkthrough

Text us photos of each building or book a walkthrough. We note bay count, ceiling heights, any lifts, outlets and heat, and what the property stores across a full year, from tack and feed to the Christmas lights.

2

Decide What Each Building Is For

Garage, shop, shed and barn each get a short list of what belongs there. This step does more for an acreage than any shelving we install.

3

Sort and Haul Away

Every item goes into keep, sell, donate, recycle or dispose. We take donations, scrap and household hazardous waste off the property so it doesn't sit in a pile behind the shop for another year.

4

Zone Design

Vehicle and machine bays, a sealed feed and garbage zone, a golf and gear station, a shop wall and long-term storage, each with the right mix of shelving, wall rails, overhead racks or custom garage storage.

5

Install, Label and Hand Off

Everything is installed, labelled and loaded, and we walk you through the system so it still works after the first big snowfall.

Golf Bags, Tack and Mountain Gear

A Golf Station by the House Door

Priddis Greens Golf and Country Club is one of two private 36-hole clubs near Calgary, and CREB's 2014 profile of the area put its membership at about 800. Households that play several times a week need the bags, push carts, shoes and a box of balls where they can grab them on the way to the car. A floor rack for two to four bags, a shoe shelf with a boot tray under it and a hook for the umbrella take about four feet of wall.

Blankets, Saddles and the Overflow From the Barn

Horse blankets that need drying, a spare saddle, grooming kits and fencing supplies often end up in the garage. Open hooks let blankets dry instead of mildewing in a pile, and labelled bins keep fencing hardware and electric-fence parts together for the next repair.

Skis, Bikes and Kayaks for the West

From Highway 22 the mountains, Bragg Creek's trails and Kananaskis are close, so most Priddis garages hold skis, mountain bikes, a canoe or kayak and camping bins. Bikes go on wall hooks, skis on a rack by the overhead door, boats on padded wall cradles or a ceiling hoist, and camping totes on the overhead rack until June.

Gravel Roads, Chinooks and Foothills Winters

Grit From Rural Roads

Many Priddis acreages are reached by rural roads and long private driveways off Highway 22 or 22X. Vehicles bring in gravel, mud and road sand all winter, and it ends up in every bin left on the floor. Shelving on legs, wall rails and overhead racks keep stored items above it, and a sealed floor makes the slab easy to squeegee.

Freeze, Thaw, Refreeze

A chinook can lift the temperature above zero in an afternoon and drop it again overnight. Snow packed in wheel wells melts across the slab and refreezes, cardboard on the floor soaks it up, and tools stored low begin to rust. In-floor heat dries a garage faster, but only if the floor is clear enough for the water to run to the drain.

Power Outages and the Backup Kit

Rural households tend to keep a generator, extension cords, fuel, headlamps and spare water on hand for winter storms. We give that kit one labelled shelf near a door so it can be found by flashlight, with the generator parked where it can be rolled straight outside. Our garage floor cleaning service can clear years of oil and road salt before the system goes in.

Frequently Asked Questions About Garage Organization in Priddis

Do you travel to Priddis?

Yes. Priddis is about 10 kilometres west of Calgary's city limits, and we schedule it on our southwest Calgary routes along with Discovery Ridge and the acreages off Highway 22 and 22X. Quotes are free, and a few photos sent by text are usually enough to start.

How much does garage organization cost on a Priddis acreage?

Garage organization starts at $199 for a standard garage. Acreage jobs that include a detached shop, a full clear-out with removal or custom cabinetry cost more depending on size and finish. We give a free, fixed written quote before any work begins.

Can you organize the shop and the garage in the same visit?

Yes, and we recommend it. Deciding what belongs in each building at the same time stops tools, fuel and seasonal gear from drifting between them. Larger properties sometimes need a second visit for installation.

How should we store feed, pet food and garbage with cougars and bears around?

Alberta's BearSmart guide lists garbage, pet food, bird seed, barbecues, grain and livestock feed as attractants. We put feed and seed in sealed bins, keep pet food on one shelf inside, store garbage carts away from the overhead door, and give the cleaned barbecue its own spot in the garage.

We have a vehicle lift. Can we still use overhead storage?

Yes, over the bays that never lift. We map the lift's full rise and the garage door opener's travel first, then place overhead racks where they won't interfere.

Where should fuel and chemicals go on an acreage?

Together, labelled, low and away from heaters and the house door, ideally in a detached shop rather than the attached garage. We group them on a metal shelf or in a cabinet and take old or unlabelled product for proper disposal.

Do you work on Priddis Greens homes too?

Yes. Those garages are closer to a city layout, so the work is usually a golf station, cabinets that match the house, overhead racks for seasonal bins and a third bay with one defined purpose.

How long does an acreage garage project take?

A single attached garage usually takes 4 to 8 hours in one visit. A garage plus a heated shop, or a full clear-out before installation, often takes two visits. We confirm the schedule in your free consultation.
