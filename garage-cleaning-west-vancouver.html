What Makes West Vancouver Different

West Vancouver shares the North Shore's climate — high rainfall driven by orographic lift, dense conifer canopy, heavy moss on aprons and door lines, and steep lots that put many garages under the house with a saturated slope behind one wall. Everything we say about North Vancouver's moisture applies here.

What's different is the scale of the garages and the nature of the work.

Larger, more complex structures

Multi-bay garages are the norm in the British Properties, Chartwell, Caulfeild and Whitby Estates rather than the exception. Three and four bays, sometimes with a workshop bay, sometimes with a car lift, often with a separate storage room. Larger doesn't mean easier — it means more contents, longer days, and more decisions.

Estate and downsizing work

A significant share of West Vancouver jobs come from downsizing, or from executors handling a property after a death. That work needs a completely different pace from a standard cleanout, and we run it differently:

  • Nothing leaves without being seen. Not a single box goes in the truck unopened.
  • Documents and photographs are separated and set aside. These surface constantly in garages and they're the things families most regret losing.
  • Possible valuables are flagged, not donated. Tools, sporting equipment, collections and older furniture get set aside for appraisal rather than assumed worthless.
  • We work to executor timelines, and we're used to coordinating with realtors where a listing date is driving the schedule.
  • Phased and stoppable. Estate jobs are quoted in stages so you can stop after any one of them.

Strata parkades in the newer buildings

West Vancouver's newer developments come with assigned parkade stalls and storage lockers, and the storage rules there are enforced more actively than most owners expect. A well-publicised West Vancouver case saw residents told they couldn't store household possessions in enclosed storage units within their own parking garage — and the analysis turned on the building's original design compliance under the BC Building and Fire Codes, not on tidiness. We clean individual stalls and lockers and flag anything likely to draw a notice.

Pre-listing

Given West Vancouver property values, pre-listing work is a meaningful part of what we do. A garage that smells musty at an open house reads to a buyer as deferred maintenance and a possible water problem — exactly the conversation nobody wants during subject removal. We turn these around fast and we're used to working backwards from a photography date.

On estate work, we'd rather be slow

The fastest way to finish an estate garage is to skip the contents wholesale. We don't do that. Photographs, documents, letters, tools and small personal items turn up in garage boxes constantly, and once they're gone they're gone. If that means the job takes two days instead of one, it takes two days — and we'll quote it that way up front rather than surprising you.

Garage Types We Work On in West Vancouver

Multi-bay estate garage

British Properties, Chartwell, Whitby Estates, Caulfeild. Three or more bays, often with a workshop area, storage room or car lift. Large contents volume and long days — usually quoted as a phased job.

Under-house garage on a steep lot

Common throughout West Vancouver's hillside. One wall holding back wet ground, shared air with living space above, and drainage that's often decades old. The uphill wall is where we look first.

Older Ambleside and Dundarave garages

Smaller, closer to the waterfront, frequently detached and often pre-war. Heavy moss exposure, original porous slabs that need low-pressure methods rather than a full-pressure wash.

Strata parkade stall and locker

The newer Ambleside and Park Royal-adjacent buildings. We clean individual assigned stalls and lockers and flag anything likely to breach strata bylaws or the BC Fire Code. Whole-building parkade scrubbing is a commercial contract we refer out.

Disposal & Drive Times from West Vancouver

West Vancouver is the longest disposal run in our Metro Vancouver service area. The North Shore Transfer Station in North Vancouver handles most loads; anything going to the Vancouver Landfill in Delta is a substantial round trip. We're upfront about that rather than hiding it in the quote.

It's also why sorting properly pays for itself most clearly here. Every item routed to donation, reuse or a free BC stewardship depot — paint, motor oil, antifreeze, electronics, batteries, tyres — is one that doesn't make the trip and doesn't attract a tipping fee. On a large West Vancouver estate garage that difference is measured in hundreds of dollars.

You get itemised disposal receipts and a summary of what was donated, recycled and landfilled. Confirm current facility hours and rates before hauling your own load; full 2026 fee detail is on our garage cleanout page.

West Vancouver Garage Services — FAQs

Do you handle estate garage clearances in West Vancouver?

Yes, and it's a large share of our work here. Estate jobs run at a different pace: nothing leaves without being seen, documents and photographs are separated and set aside rather than binned, and items of possible value are flagged for appraisal rather than donated by default. We work to executor timelines, coordinate with realtors where a listing date drives the schedule, and quote in phases so you can stop after any stage.

How much does a West Vancouver garage cleanout cost?

A single garage clean starts at $249 and a double at $349. Multi-bay estate garages are quoted on site and typically start around $1,200, running higher with volume. Estate and downsizing clearances are phased. Roughly 60 to 70 percent of a cleanout total is labour and the rest is disposal, which we pass through at cost with receipts rather than marking up.

Can you work on a three or four bay garage?

Yes — multi-bay garages are the norm in the British Properties, Chartwell, Caulfeild and Whitby Estates rather than the exception. Larger doesn't mean simpler: more contents, more decisions and longer days. We quote these as phased jobs so the scope and the stopping points are clear before we start.

Why does my West Vancouver garage smell musty?

Usually one of two things. If it's an under-house garage on a slope, water is likely entering at the uphill wall where wet ground sits behind it — and that wall is normally the darkest, least visited part of the garage, so growth establishes before anyone notices. Otherwise it's general slab moisture with no ventilation. Either way the smell is microbial, meaning there's active growth somewhere. We find the source and treat it rather than masking it.

Can I store household items in my strata parkade locker?

Check your bylaws first. Stratas can restrict what's kept in garages and parking areas, and the BC Fire Code restricts combustible storage in parking structures independently of that. A well-publicised West Vancouver case saw residents told they couldn't store household possessions in enclosed storage units in their own parking garage, and it turned on the building's original design compliance rather than tidiness. We clean individual stalls and lockers and flag anything likely to draw a notice.

Do you work in Lions Bay and Horseshoe Bay?

Yes, along with Eagle Harbour, Gleneagles and Whytecliff. These are the longest drives in our service area so we prefer to schedule them efficiently — if you're out that way, call and ask about availability rather than assuming. Travel time is factored into the quote and we tell you so rather than burying it.
