Half a Lot, Half a Lane

Altadore runs from 33 Avenue SW down to 50 Avenue, between 14 Street and Crowchild Trail, with River Park and the Elbow on its east edge. It was established in 1945, and it belongs to the Marda Loop Communities Association together with Garrison Woods and South Calgary. The shops on 33 and 34 Avenues give the area its everyday name.

City census figures from 2018 counted 3,218 dwellings in Altadore, not including Garrison Woods. Only 38% of them were detached houses. Another 30% were semi-detached or duplexes, and 29% of all dwellings were rented. Many of the semis were built in the last 15 years as the older bungalows were replaced, and the listings read the way you'd expect: "semi detached (half duplex)", "double detached garage", "back lane". Some describe the garage as insulated, drywalled and heated.

So a garage cleanout here is often a job on one half of something. Two garages stand side by side on the lane, or one building is split by a wall, and the person next door has a car, a bin and a schedule of their own. That shapes how we plan the day more than the size of the garage does.

When a Tenant Left It Behind

Close to a third of Altadore homes are rented, so a garage full of a former tenant's things is a common reason to need one cleared here. The house gets cleaned, the keys come back, and the garage still holds a mattress, a weight bench and a dozen boxes. In Alberta, what you can do with those things depends on what they're worth. Section 31 of the Residential Tenancies Act, as the Government of Alberta summarizes it:

  • If you believe the goods are worth less than $2,000, or they'd lose much of their value sitting in storage, you can dispose of them right away.
  • If they're worth $2,000 or more, you have to store them for 30 days.
  • The tenant can get them back during that time by paying the moving and storage costs.
  • If nobody claims them after 30 days, you can sell them by public auction, or by private sale with the court's approval.

The value judgment is yours as the landlord, not ours. What we can do is make the garage easy to judge and leave you a record of it. This is the order we work in on a rental:

  1. Photograph the garage before anything moves. Wide shots of each wall and the rafters, sent to you the same day. If the tenancy ends in a dispute, you have a picture of what was there.
  2. Pull out anything that could be worth real money. Tools, a newer bike, sports gear, a safe or a locked case, anything with a serial number. These go into a separate pile and get photographed again on their own.
  3. You decide on the separate pile. If you want it stored, we can move it to a storage unit you've rented. If you've decided it's under $2,000, it goes with the rest.
  4. Papers and IDs stay with you. Mail, tax slips, passports and photos never go in a donation bag or a garbage load. We hand them over sealed.
  5. The rest is sorted and hauled. Donation, the hazardous waste drop-off, then the landfill, and the floor is swept for the next tenant's walkthrough.

If you're unsure what the tenant's things are worth, or whether the tenancy has actually ended, check the Government of Alberta's RTA handbook or ask a lawyer before you book. You Just Got the Keys and the Garage Isn't Empty

When a house here changes hands, some buyers open the garage on possession day to find paint cans, offcuts, an old fridge or a stack of tiles the seller decided were "for the house". Before you call anyone to haul it, do these three things:

  1. Photograph it with the date. Before you or a mover puts anything else in there.
  2. Call your realtor. Your purchase contract says what the seller had to remove and what was meant to stay. That's between your realtor and theirs, and the photos help.
  3. Keep the matching materials. Leftover flooring, siding, shingles or paint labelled with a colour code can match repairs on a newer house. Set those aside before deciding the rest is junk.

Then send us the photos. We can usually clear a possession-day garage before your movers need it, and our same-day garage cleanout page explains how short-notice bookings work. What Changes an Altadore Price

We price from your photos, or from a walkthrough on a big garage, and agree the price before the truck is booked. These are the things that move it on jobs here:

  • Your half, or the whole building. A single bay on a split garage is a different job from both bays, even when the outside looks the same.
  • A separate pile for a tenant. Photographing, listing and moving goods to storage takes longer than loading everything together.
  • The lane. A truck at your door is quicker than parking on the street and carrying through the yard, and some Altadore lanes are tight with two garages facing each other.
  • Weight on the scale. Tile, concrete, old exercise equipment and hardened paint are heavy for their size.
  • Refrigerant appliances. Each one has its own landfill fee.
  • Rafters and finished walls. A drywalled, heated garage gets more care moving things past the walls and the heater than a bare stud one does.
  • A fixed date. A possession day or a new tenant's move-in can mean a Saturday or a second crew.

Altadore and Marda Loop Garage Cleanout Questions

How long do I have to keep a tenant's belongings in Alberta?

Under section 31 of Alberta's Residential Tenancies Act, a landlord can dispose of goods right away if they believe the goods are worth less than $2,000, or if the goods would lose much of their value in storage. Goods worth $2,000 or more have to be stored for 30 days. The tenant can reclaim them by paying the moving and storage costs. After 30 days, the landlord can sell them by public auction, or by private sale with the court's approval. The value call is the landlord's, so check the Government of Alberta's RTA handbook or a lawyer if you're unsure. The tenant section above shows how we keep the two piles apart.

Can you clear my half of a shared double garage without touching my neighbour's things?

Yes. Before the day, tell us where your side ends: the wall, the post, or a line on the floor. On the day we only load what you've pointed to, and anything we can't place goes into a marked pile for you to check with next door. We keep the lane in front of their door clear while we load.

How much does it cost to clean out a garage in Altadore?

We price each garage from photos, or a walkthrough for a big one, and agree the price before booking. The things that move it are volume, weight that has to go over the landfill scale, fridges and freezers, rafter storage, whether we're keeping a tenant's goods apart for storage, and whether the truck can reach the door from the lane. Shelving and storage installs after the cleanout are quoted separately by our garage organization team.

What is the cheapest way to get rid of junk from a Marda Loop garage?

Keep the free streams separate. Paint, solvents, propane cylinders under 30 lb and other household hazardous waste go free to the City drop-off at Lincoln Park/Garrison Green Fire Station #20, 2800 Peacekeepers Way SW, open 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. every day. Tires and electronics are free at the landfill when they're kept apart from garbage. Only mixed garbage pays the scale rate.

How much does it cost to use the dump in Calgary?

The City of Calgary's 2026 residential rate is a flat $25 for a load under 250 kg. Heavier loads are weighed and charged at $113 a tonne for basic garbage, and each fridge, freezer or air conditioner adds a $17 refrigerant fee. City landfills are closed on Sundays.

How do I get rid of garage clutter if I own a rental and live elsewhere?

Send us photos and the access details: a lockbox, a door code or a key with a neighbour. We do a video walkthrough with you, clear the garage, photograph anything set aside, and send you the photos and a list before the truck leaves the lane. You don't need to be in Calgary.

Are you insured if something gets damaged in a finished garage?

Yes. GarageScape carries $2 million in general liability insurance, and we can send the certificate to you, your property manager or your condo board before the job.
