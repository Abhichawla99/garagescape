How we book same-day garage jobs

A garage full of junk, cleared the same day you call. That promise only works if the phone system behind it never drops a ball. Here's ours, told honestly.

Quick answer

GarageScape books same-day garage cleanouts across Calgary, Airdrie and Okotoks by being phone-first: you call or text, a real person answers, and your slot is booked on that first call. Our team works every request through DialSheet, a power dialer and CRM built for sales teams, so callbacks never slip and every homeowner hears back the same day. Same-day is a phone problem

When somebody decides to clear the garage, they usually want it gone this week — often today. Half the battle is being the company that answers. The other half is the follow-up: the customer who texts a photo at 9pm and asks "can you do Saturday?" needs an answer before somebody else's truck shows up.

Our team runs every request — inbound calls, texts with photos, quote follow-ups — through one organized system, so nothing sits in a voicemail box. When a crew finishes a job early, the next booking is already queued.

What you experience as a customer

You call or text. A real person answers, asks two or three quick questions (roughly how full, is there anything heavy, what's your timeline), and gives you a price range on the spot. If you like the number, your slot is booked before we hang up — morning, afternoon, or the exact window you need on a Saturday.

Miss our call-back and we try again at a decent hour — never a barrage. Say "just an email" and that's what you get. Every note from every conversation stays on your file, so whoever shows up at your door already knows what you told us on the phone.
